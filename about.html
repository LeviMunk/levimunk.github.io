03 / BEYOND THE RESUME

A little more about me.

I'm Levi Van Stappen. I grew up in Wisconsin's Fox Valley, and while my professional path has crossed a few different fields, the things I enjoy outside work tell a pretty consistent story about who I am.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Curious about people, ideas, and how things work.

I've never been especially interested in staying in one lane. Teaching, running a business, creating things, exploring technology, and bringing people together have all been meaningful parts of my life.

That curiosity shows up in my professional work, too. I like learning how different pieces fit together and finding practical ways to make an experience better, whether it's for a customer, a team, or a group of friends.

SOME THINGS THAT MATTER TO ME

Outside the job title

01 / ROOTS

The Fox Valley has always been home.

I grew up in the Fox Valley, and this area has shaped a lot of my life. It's where I've built relationships, worked with local communities, and eventually opened my own studio. I value the familiarity of a place where people and connections matter.

02 / MARTIAL ARTS

Nearly 25 years on the mats.

Martial arts have been part of my life for almost 25 years. Over that time I've been a student, an instructor, and the owner of a jiu-jitsu studio. Teaching taught me how differently people learn, how to build trust, and how much a welcoming community can mean to someone.

03 / COMMUNITY

Dungeons & Dragons is about more than the game.

I'm a strong advocate for Dungeons & Dragons and the community it creates. I love bringing people around a table, helping newcomers feel comfortable, and creating shared stories people remember long after a session ends. It's part storytelling, part collaboration, and a great excuse to make new friends.

04 / HERITAGE

Keeping my Danish roots close.

My Danish heritage is an important part of who I am. I've visited Denmark many times, and those trips have given me a deeper appreciation for my family's history and traditions. One way I stay connected to that heritage at home is through Scandinavian baking, especially learning the techniques behind traditional pastries.

05 / OUTDOORS

Getting outside with Nimbus.

When I'm not making something or running a game, I enjoy hiking and exploring with Nimbus, my golden retriever. A good trail, some fresh air, and a very enthusiastic dog are a pretty great way to spend the day.

06 / MAKING THINGS

My curiosity usually turns into a project.

I enjoy experimenting with personal automation, from connecting systems around my apartment to building a fully digital tabletop interface for my D&D games. I like the challenge of making hardware, software, and everyday routines work together. For me, the satisfying part is turning an idea into something people can actually use.

HOW IT ALL CONNECTS

A broader perspective makes me a better problem-solver.

Whether I'm coaching, creating, building a system, or organizing a group experience, I keep coming back to the same things: understand what people need, make the process clearer, and build something worthwhile.
