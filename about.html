Outside the job title

The Fox Valley has always been home.

The Fox Valley has shaped much of my life. It's where I built relationships, worked with local communities, and opened my studio.

I value the kind of place where connections last.

Nearly 25 years on the mats.

Nearly 25 years in martial arts took me from student to instructor to studio owner.

Teaching showed me how differently people learn, and how important a welcoming community can be.

Dungeons & Dragons is about more than the game.

I love bringing people around a table to create stories together, especially when someone is playing for the first time.

For me, D&D is as much about collaboration and community as it is about the game.

Keeping my Danish roots close.

I've visited Denmark many times, deepening my connection to my family's history and traditions.

At home, Scandinavian baking is one of the ways I keep that heritage close.

Getting outside with Nimbus.

Hiking with Nimbus, my golden retriever, is one of my favorite ways to spend a day.

A good trail, some fresh air, and an enthusiastic dog are hard to beat.

My curiosity usually turns into a project.

I build personal automation projects, from connected apartment systems to a digital interface for my D&D table.

The interesting challenge is making hardware, software, and daily routines work together.

A broader perspective makes me a better problem-solver.

Whether I'm coaching, creating, building a system, or organizing a group experience, I keep coming back to the same things: understand what people need, make the process clearer, and build something worthwhile.
