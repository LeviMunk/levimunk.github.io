# CleaningOS — premium prospect showcase (11.18h)

## Intent

Make the **public prospect experience** feel like an elegant, purpose-built business operating system, while the real **workspace** remains fast and practical. Someone saying yes to an outreach message should receive a tailored visual demonstration, not an unsolicited account-setup form.

## Implemented

- Public entry `/cleaning-os/?start=welcome` now opens a forest-green, editorial first-visit sales showcase with a real clickable demo handoff, example operational data card, guided journey CTA, and interactive opportunity selector.
- Opportunity cards:
  - `rebooking`: open Elevate / customer retention
  - `leads`: open FrontDoor / lead management
  - `operations`: open Flow / service operations
- `/cleaning-os/?start=builder` opens a lightweight prospect-link builder. It encodes a **public business name** and selected focus into a shareable URL, e.g. `/cleaning-os/?start=welcome&company=Example%20Cleaning%20Co&focus=leads`. No server records, private keys, tracking or tokenized invitations.
- The public URL shows a company-named welcome message and opens the **existing sample business workspace**. A banner there explicitly identifies the brand context and warns that records, customers and numbers are fictional. The real demo company data does not become the prospect's actual data.
- Website uses responsive layouts with reduced-motion preference support. Premium typography, polished compositions, restricted dark-green palette and clear call-to-action hierarchy are restricted to the sales showcase.
- Retained local setup draft under All Tools → Business Setup (no actual account or OAuth).
- Existing saved-demo visitors still land on Command Center by default, with topbar "View Showcase" and sidebar "SHOWCASE" available.

## Example prospect workflow

1. You identify a cleaning-business prospect and a specific business opportunity from authorized/public information.
2. Open the demo-link builder, enter company name, choose rebooking, lead conversion or service operations, and generate a URL.
3. Send the URL in a personalized outreach message when appropriate; no automation is enabled.
4. Prospect explores the branded showcase and enters a relevant fictional demo, with explicit disclaimers.
5. If the prospect wants help, they use the Levi Munk portfolio contact route; no account is created, and no fake demo-analytics events are recorded.
6. Only after a real agreement, route the client into secure MunkOS-backed onboarding.

## Safety, scope and limits

- The URL isn't authentication or confidentiality. It should be treated as a **public** link and should only contain a public business name and topic.
- It does not fetch website content, copy a brand logo, create integrations, publish a website, send outreach, provision a tenant, track visits, or disclose client records.
- Example metrics (12 visits planned, 4 follow-ups, 8 repeat customers) are **staged fictional examples**, not analytics for the prospect.
- The prospect's branded name only affects the showcase and contextual banner. It does not modify original seeded CleaningOS demo records.
- Public portfolio is an outbound point of contact; no CRM lead is generated by opening it.
- The hosted demo and the local setup wizard stay clearly distinct from paid client environments.
