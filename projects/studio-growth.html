From first class to belonging.

How do you turn an interested visitor into a person who feels welcome enough to return?

PROJECT CONTEXT

THEØRY Jiu-Jitsu · Founder and operator

Build the full member experience

MY ROLEAudience strategy + studio operations
EVIDENCE~21 to 40 membersObserved June–September 2023

The challenge

Not everyone considering a studio wanted the same thing. Some wanted fitness, confidence, community, or a new challenge. A one-size-fits-all message missed those motivations.

The strategy I built

MotivationCommunity · Fitness · Confidence
InquiryGoals, interests, and questions
Free trialWelcoming first experience
Early visitsFollow-up and attendance
MembershipConnection and continuity

The marketing message was about the outcome someone wanted in life, not only about learning martial arts.

  • Audience motivationsCreated messaging for people interested in fitness, community, empowerment, confidence, and personal growth, not just people already looking for martial arts.
  • Trial fitUsed inquiry questions and early conversations to understand goals and align expectations with the trial experience.
  • Zen PlannerUsed Zen Planner as the studio's main CRM to coordinate prospect and member information.
  • Email + textCreated email campaigns and drip follow-ups through email and SMS across inquiries, trials, and early membership.
  • Early attendanceNoticed that people who returned for more early classes seemed more likely to stay; this was an operating observation, not a calculated retention threshold.
  • Member experienceConnected website messaging, onboarding, schedules, and day-to-day communications with the studio experience.

I also bring 10+ years of WordPress experience. That experience is not presented as a measured outcome of THEØRY.

Evidence & outcomes

Membership was approximately 21 in June 2023 and 40 in September 2023, an observed increase of around 90%. These are historical estimates, not an audited CRM export, and no individual campaign can be credited with that change.

What I observed, but didn't formally quantify

People who attended several classes early on appeared more likely to remain members. I cannot publish a defensible visit cutoff or a measured retention rate from the records available.

What I learned

Getting someone to try a class was only the beginning. Follow-through and the experience after the first visit mattered just as much.

How I would measure it today

Lead source, trial show rate, visits in the first 30 days, email/SMS response, 30/60/90-day retention, and cancellation reasons. These are suggested future measurements, not historical results.

Portfolio transparency: this is a founder-led business case. The membership numbers are approximate historical observations, not verified campaign attribution. The early-attendance pattern was noticed in practice, not established by a formal cohort study.
