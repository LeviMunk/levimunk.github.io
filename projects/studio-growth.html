THE WORK, EXPLAINED

How do you turn an interested visitor into a person who feels welcome enough to return?

The challenge

People contacted the studio with different goals and levels of experience. A generic process would not account for those differences.

What I contributed

  • Managed positioning, lead intake, website content, and the free-trial experience.
  • Organized inquiry questions to capture goals, interests, and experience.
  • Connected marketing messages with first-class expectations, onboarding, and member communications.
  • Balanced member acquisition with operations and the day-to-day customer experience.

Evidence & outcomes

Membership increased from approximately 21 to 40 between June and September 2023, around 90% growth over that period. This is descriptive growth and cannot be attributed to a single initiative.

What I learned

Acquisition is only one part of a business. Clear handoffs and a good first experience matter too.
