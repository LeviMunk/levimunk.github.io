THE WORK, EXPLAINED

How do you make something specialized easy to understand and interesting to watch?

The challenge

Technical products and community experiences often need more than a description. People need to see processes, examples, and the human side of the work.

What I contributed

  • Developed video concepts, filmed footage, edited short-form content, and wrote captions.
  • Created product education, artist collaborations, manufacturing-process stories, studio content, and event promotion.
  • Managed social calendars, creative assets, publishing, and approval coordination.
  • Reviewed audience response to help refine future messaging and formats.

Evidence & outcomes

The work demonstrates hands-on creative production and consistent execution. Individual work samples have not yet been linked because their public URLs and permissions need to be verified.

What I learned

Creative production works best when the visual idea helps the audience understand something useful.

Portfolio transparency: project descriptions identify my individual work. Numbers are contextualized. Work samples will be linked when public URLs and permissions are verified.
