Education before the inquiry.

How can a website support a more informed decision before someone requests a consultation?

PROJECT CONTEXT

Healthcare marketing support

All projects ↗

Make the decision easier to understand

MY ROLEEducational website messaging
EVIDENCEEducation-first approachNo verified conversion lift

The challenge

Healthcare decisions can feel uncertain. Service information needs to be clear, trustworthy, and helpful rather than simply pushing for a form submission.

What I contributed

  • Clearer messagingSupported clearer website messaging and educational information.
  • Visitor questionsFramed content around questions people may have before a consultation.
  • Trust & next stepsConsidered the relationship between trust, understanding, and the next useful action.

Evidence & outcomes

The work supported an education-first approach. No verified conversion lift or revenue outcome is attributed to this project.

What I learned

Clarity is part of the customer experience, especially when the decision feels consequential.
