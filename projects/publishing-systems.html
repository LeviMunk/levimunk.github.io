THE WORK, EXPLAINED

How do you reduce repetitive coordination while keeping people in control?

The challenge

When planning, approval, and publishing live in different places, it becomes hard to tell what is ready, blocked, approved, or complete.

What I contributed

  • Designed a structured editorial queue and explicit publishing stages.
  • Introduced an approval gate before publishing content.
  • Connected status, ownership, follow-up actions, and publishing history.
  • Focused on dependable handoffs instead of unattended automation.

Evidence & outcomes

An approval-gated workflow designed to make status and accountability easier to track. No measured time-savings figure is claimed.

What I learned

Good automation reduces friction without removing judgment or responsibility.
