THE WORK, EXPLAINED

What can content metrics tell us, and what can’t they?

The challenge

High reach and engagement can make a campaign appear successful, but those metrics do not reveal downstream inquiries, orders, or business impact without additional measurement.

What I contributed

  • Monitored reach, engagement, content response, and recurring audience patterns.
  • Used available reporting to evaluate content formats, messaging, and educational storytelling.
  • Separated awareness metrics from conversion and revenue questions requiring additional data.
  • Brought observations into campaign planning and iteration.

Evidence & outcomes

One fine-art materials reel exceeded 115,000 views. That is a reach result, not a claim of attributable sales, conversion improvement, or ROI.

What I learned

Start with a decision worth making, then choose measurements that can actually inform it.
