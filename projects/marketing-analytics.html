Reading reach without mistaking it for revenue

MY ROLEContent performance analysis
EVIDENCE115K+ views on one reelReach; not attributed revenue

The challenge

High reach and engagement can look like success, but don't establish inquiries, orders, or business impact without additional measurement.

What I contributed

  • Audience signalsMonitored reach, engagement, content response, and recurring audience patterns.
  • Content reviewUsed available reporting to evaluate content formats, messaging, and educational storytelling.
  • Measurement limitsSeparated awareness metrics from conversion and revenue questions requiring additional data.
  • Strategy feedbackBrought observations into campaign planning and iteration.

Evidence & outcomes

One fine-art materials reel exceeded 115,000 views. That is a reach result, not a claim of attributable sales, conversion improvement, or ROI.
